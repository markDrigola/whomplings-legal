Information We Collect

When you download and use the Game, we and our service providers may collect:

  • Device & technical data — device model, operating system and version, language, country, IP address, and app performance and diagnostic data.
  • Gameplay and analytics data — anonymous analytics events describing how the Game is played, such as launches, session length, matches started and finished, level reached, and rewards received, together with an anonymous installation identifier generated by Unity Gaming Services.
  • Advertising identifiers — your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) and related identifiers, used to deliver and measure ads (see "Advertising").
  • Purchase data — records of in-app purchases (transaction identifiers and what was purchased). Payment card details are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.
  • Online multiplayer data — when you play Battle Net: a nickname you choose or that the Game generates for you, a random player identifier created on your device, your IP address and connection quality, the game version you are running, and the in-match actions needed to keep every player's screen in step. See "Online Multiplayer" below.

Your game progress stays on your device. This holds for online play too — Battle Net matches are not saved anywhere, and nothing about how you played is kept once the room closes. Your heroes, currency, level, daily rewards, and settings are stored locally in the Game's own storage. We do not operate player accounts and we do not keep a copy of your progress on our servers.

We do not intentionally collect your real name, email address, precise location (GPS), contacts, photos, or other special categories of personal data.

How We Use Information

We use the information to: operate and maintain the Game; understand how the Game is played so we can improve balance and difficulty; deliver remote configuration values such as prices and reward amounts without shipping an update; process in-app purchases; show and measure advertising; provide support; ensure security and prevent fraud; and comply with legal obligations.

Advertising

The Game is supported by advertising, including optional rewarded video ads that grant in-game rewards for watching. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay mediation and its ad networks, which include Unity Ads, ironSource, and other mediated advertising partners. These partners may collect and process device and advertising identifiers to select, deliver, frequency-cap, and measure ads.

  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS 14.5 and later, we ask for your permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking. If you do not grant permission, ads are still shown but are non-personalized, and measurement relies on Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: We ask for your consent before analytics and advertising services start: you accept the Terms and this Policy on first launch, and on iOS the ATT prompt separately governs access to your advertising identifier. Where consent for personalized ads has not been given, ads are shown non-personalized. You can withdraw your consent at any time in your device's tracking settings, or by contacting us at the address below.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings.
  • The Game also offers an optional purchase that removes forced ads. Buying it does not change what data our advertising partners have already processed.

Online Multiplayer

The Game's Battle Net mode connects you to a shared match with other players. This is optional: the rest of the Game works fully offline, and no multiplayer data is processed until you enter the mode.

  • Who operates it — multiplayer is powered by Photon, operated by Exit Games GmbH (Germany). Photon runs the servers that relay the match. Exit Games acts as our processor and we remain the controller of your data, so questions and requests about multiplayer data go to us, not to them. See the Photon Privacy & Cookie Policy.
  • What Photon receives — your IP address (unavoidable for any online connection), a random identifier generated on your device, your chosen nickname, the version of the Game you are running, and the moment-to-moment actions of your character during the match.
  • What other players see — your nickname and your character's actions during the match. Please do not use a nickname containing your real name, your email address, or anything else you would not want other players to see. You can change your nickname at any time in the Game's settings.
  • The identifier — a random value created on your device the first time you play online, with no connection to your device's advertising identifier or to any account. It exists so that a player whose connection drops can return to the same match instead of arriving as a stranger. Deleting the Game deletes it.
  • Where it is processed — Photon selects the nearest available region automatically, so a match may be relayed by servers outside your own country. See "International Data Transfers".
  • How long it is kept — match data lives only for the duration of the room and is not stored by us afterwards. Photon retains connection logs under its own policy.
  • Voice and chat — the Game has none. There is no way to send text or speech to another player.

In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases of virtual currency and of an ad-free period. Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive a confirmation that a purchase succeeded and what it contained; we never receive or store your payment card details.

Because purchases unlock content stored on your device, deleting the Game may cause purchased virtual items to be lost. See the Terms of Service for details.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Game is not a website and does not use browser cookies. Our service providers use SDK-level identifiers and similar technologies inside the app for the purposes described above.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Unity Technologies (which includes ironSource) — Unity Gaming Services (analytics, remote configuration) and Unity LevelPlay (advertising mediation). See the Unity Privacy Policy and the Unity Player & App User Privacy Policy.
  • Mediated ad networks — to deliver and measure ads through LevelPlay mediation.
  • Exit Games GmbH (Photon) — online multiplayer matchmaking and match relay, when you play Battle Net. See the Photon Privacy & Cookie Policy.
  • Other players in your match — your nickname and your character's actions, for as long as the match lasts.
  • Apple and Google — app distribution, in-app purchases, and crash reporting. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Authorities — when required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, and property.

International Data Transfers

We and our service providers operate internationally, and your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, agreed with our processors.

Data Retention

Analytics and advertising data are retained by our service providers for the periods set out in their own policies, typically no longer than is necessary for the purposes described here. Purchase records are retained as long as required by tax and accounting law. Because your game progress lives on your device, it is retained until you delete it or uninstall the Game.

Data Deletion / Opt-Out

  • Delete your game data: uninstall the Game. This permanently removes your progress, currency, and unlocked heroes from your device. We cannot restore them.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: decline the ATT prompt on iOS, or turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" on Android. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, change your choice from the Game's settings screen.
  • Stay out of online play: simply do not enter Battle Net. Every other mode runs entirely on your device, and no multiplayer data is sent.
  • Delete your multiplayer data: changing your nickname in the Game's settings replaces it everywhere it is used from then on, and uninstalling the Game deletes the random player identifier from your device. For anything already in Photon's connection logs, write to mdgametechnology@gmail.com and we will instruct our processor to delete it. It helps to tell us the nickname you played under and roughly when.
  • Request deletion of analytics data: write to mdgametechnology@gmail.com and we will ask our service providers to delete the analytics and advertising data associated with your installation. We may ask you for details that help them locate it, such as your device model and the approximate date you installed the Game.
